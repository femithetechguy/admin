### **Situation**
When I joined Manhattan Associates, the IT and Operations teams were struggling with a lack of data visibility. We had multiple data sources, but there was no unified way to see and analyze performance metrics across departments. This led to inefficient reporting and a fragmented view of key business functions.

---

### **Task**
My primary task was to transform the company's reporting capabilities. I needed to build a comprehensive, end-to-end business intelligence framework that would provide scalable, real-time insights for both IT and operations, while also improving overall platform reliability and user adoption.

---

### **Action**
I took a leadership role in developing the BI solutions. I **architected an end-to-end BI solution using Microsoft Fabric**, which provided the foundation for scalable and real-time reporting. To consolidate our data, I built **data ingestion pipelines** using Power Query, Dataflows, and Fabric Pipelines, pulling information from key sources like **Jira, ServiceNow, and Oracle** into SQL Server.

From there, I developed over **20 advanced Power BI dashboards** for IT and operations, which significantly improved cross-departmental data visibility. I also focused on performance, **optimizing semantic models, calculation groups, and DAX logic** to make sure our reports were fast and dynamic.

Beyond just development, I provided **Tier-2/3 support**, troubleshooting complex issues to maintain high platform uptime and reliability. To empower the organization, I **authored and maintained internal documentation** on Power BI governance and Fabric architecture, which enhanced platform adoption and enabled self-service BI.

---

### **Result**
The results of this work were significant. The over 20 dashboards I developed led to a **60% improvement in cross-departmental data visibility and reporting efficiency**. By architecting a scalable BI solution and optimizing performance, I not only streamlined data consolidation processes but also ensured that the platform was reliable and easy for users to adopt. My efforts laid the foundation for a data-driven culture, moving the business toward more informed and efficient decision-making.
