# Technical Skills for 5-8 Years of Experience

The provided image outlines the advanced skills and technical knowledge required for a BI professional with 5-8 years of experience. This level is a significant step up, demanding a deep, architectural understanding of the entire BI ecosystem and the ability to work with enterprise-level solutions.

---

## 1. Expert Level in SQL

SQL knowledge is expected to be at an expert level, which includes a mastery of:

- **Database Concepts and Querying:** Deep understanding of database management systems, complex SQL queries, joins, views, `CTE`s, and `MERGE` statements.
- **Stored Procedures:** The ability to create and manage complex stored procedures.
- **Performance Tuning:** Expertise in optimizing queries for maximum efficiency.

---

## 2. Basic Knowledge of Data Warehousing Concepts

This role requires a solid grasp of data warehousing principles, including:

- **Dimensional Modeling:** The ability to design and implement robust dimensional models (`star` and `snowflake` schemas).
- **Fact and Dimension Tables:** A thorough understanding of how fact and dimension tables are used to store measures and attributes.
- **SCD (Slowly Changing Dimensions):** Knowledge of different types of SCDs for tracking changes in data over time.

---

## 3. ETL Tool

At this level, a professional is expected to have extensive experience with ETL tools, including:

- **SSIS and Azure Data Factory:** Expertise in using these tools to build complex ETL pipelines.
- **Control Flows and Dataflows:** Advanced skills in designing and orchestrating data movement and transformations.
- **Scheduling and Automation:** The ability to automate tasks and schedule recurring data loads.
- **Reconciliation of Reports:** Experience in validating and reconciling report data against source systems.

---

## 4. Business Intelligence

A professional with 5-8 years of experience is expected to manage the entire BI lifecycle:

- **Complete BI Module:** The ability to oversee a project from the initial requirements gathering to the final delivery and maintenance.

---

## 5. Power BI Architecture

This role requires a deep understanding of the entire Power BI ecosystem, including:

- **Power Query and Modeling:** Expert-level skills in data transformation and data modeling, including complex relationships.
- **Scheduling and Deployment:** Expertise in scheduling refreshes, managing apps, and deploying to the Power BI Service.
- **Premium Features:** Familiarity with **Power BI Premium** features like **Dataflows**, which are self-service ETL tools in the cloud.
- **On-premise Power BI:** Experience with on-premises solutions like Power BI Report Server.

---

## 6. In-depth Understanding of DAX

DAX skills should be advanced, focusing on complex calculations and performance tuning:

- **Advanced DAX:** Mastery of concepts like row context, filter context, and context transition.
- **Date Calculations:** Expertise in using time intelligence functions.
- **Performance Improvement:** The ability to write and optimize DAX for efficient report performance.

---

## 7. Modeling

Modeling skills at this level are highly refined, focusing on best practices for large-scale enterprise solutions:

- **Relationships:** A deep understanding of when to use or avoid different relationship types (many-to-many, one-to-one, two-way).
- **RLS (Row-Level Security):** The ability to implement and manage dynamic security rules.
- **Aggregations and Composite Models:** Experience with building aggregations to improve query performance on large datasets, and familiarity with **Composite Models** that combine Import and DirectQuery data.

---

## 8. SSAS and Azure Technologies

At this level, a professional must have knowledge of **SSAS (SQL Server Analysis Services)**, with the **Tabular model** being the recommended choice. Additionally, a strong understanding of the broader **Azure ecosystem** is a "good to have," including:

- **Azure SQL Database and Azure Synapse:** Experience with Azure's data storage and warehousing solutions.
- **Azure Data Factory and Azure Analysis Services:** Familiarity with Azure's native data integration and semantic modeling tools.
