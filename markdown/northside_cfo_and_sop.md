### **Situation**
At Northside Hospital, different clinical and operational departments were working with siloed data. This made it incredibly difficult for management to get a unified view of hospital performance and clinical outcomes. This lack of centralized, real-time data was leading to inefficiencies and potential delays in patient care.

---

### **Task**
My task was to create a unified analytics platform. I needed to build a robust data infrastructure and develop interactive dashboards that would provide departments with the specific, secure insights they needed to improve patient outcomes and operational efficiency.

---

### **Action**
I started by **partnering with cross-functional teams** to consolidate data. I built a seamless data integration system that pulled from a variety of sources, including **Azure services, on-premises systems, and Microsoft Fabric**. This laid the groundwork for a single source of truth. I then built **end-to-end data models** by ingesting information from diverse sources like **Lakehouse, Azure SQL, and SQL Server**. This allowed us to monitor real-time throughput, directly contributing to a reduction in patient care delays.

Using **Power BI, DAX, Power Pivot, and Power Query (M)**, I designed and developed a suite of reports and dashboards. These tools were more than just visuals; they were powerful analytical instruments. I created custom clinical and operational **scorecards** with advanced visuals, such as KPI cards and drill-throughs, to empower data-driven care decisions. For example, a doctor could drill down from a high-level patient outcome metric to see the specific data points that contributed to it.

Crucially, to handle sensitive patient information, I **implemented Row-Level Security (RLS)**. This ensured that each department could only access the data relevant to their operations, strictly maintaining data segregation and compliance with **HIPAA regulations**.

---

### **Result**
The end result was a transformational shift in how the hospital used its data. By unifying disparate data sources and providing a secure, centralized analytics platform, I gave our teams the tools they needed to make faster, more informed decisions. The real-time throughput monitoring helped us reduce patient care delays, while the custom scorecards provided critical insights that led to improved patient outcomes and more efficient hospital performance.
