### **Situation**
When I was at Hiscox, my role was to provide actionable insights for the business. I noticed a key problem: while we had a lot of data, we weren't effectively using it to inform our decisions. For example, we had a sense that some reports weren't being used, and that our underwriting and claims processes could be more efficient and profitable.

---

### **Task**
My task was to leverage business intelligence to improve efficiency and profitability. This meant I needed to build dashboards that provided clear insights into how our reports were being used, and to create tools that helped both underwriters and claims adjusters make faster, more accurate decisions. I also needed to investigate specific financial variances to help optimize our business strategy.

---

### **Action**
I developed a **usage analytics dashboard in Power BI** to track report engagement. This allowed me to see exactly which reports were being used and by whom, which helped us identify training gaps and refine our content strategy.

For our operational teams, I built **underwriting and claims dashboards** that combined historical and real-time data. This was a game-changer. It helped us enhance **fraud detection** by flagging suspicious patterns and **accelerated decision-making cycles** by providing key information at a glance.

Furthermore, I was tasked with a specific financial investigation. I delved into the **cost-to-claim ratio variances** to understand why some policies were less profitable than others. By analyzing the data, I was able to pinpoint the root causes of the variance, which directly contributed to a **$750K premium adjustment**.

Finally, to support our sales team, I built **broker performance scorecards** that incorporated critical metrics like loss ratios and bind rates. These provided key insights that directly shaped our regional sales strategies.

---

### **Result**
Through these initiatives, I was able to deliver tangible financial and operational improvements. The usage analytics dashboard improved our internal efficiency, the underwriting and claims dashboards led to better decisions and faster cycle times, and the cost-to-claim ratio analysis resulted in a significant premium adjustment that directly improved profitability. The broker scorecards gave us a clear, data-driven way to improve our sales strategies. In short, I used data to move the business from guesswork to informed action.
