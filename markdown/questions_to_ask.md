# Questions to Ask in Your Interview

It's a great sign that you're meeting with both the CFO and the S&OP Director—it shows they value the strategic impact of a data/BI role. Here are some questions to ask, framed to demonstrate your strategic thinking and passion for the business.

## Questions for the CFO

These questions focus on financial impact, strategic alignment, and the role of data in decision-making at the highest level.

- "What are the biggest financial challenges or opportunities you see for the company in the next 1-2 years, and how do you envision this role contributing directly to addressing them?"
- "Beyond standard financial reports, what are the key performance indicators (KPIs) or data insights that you personally use to drive high-level financial strategy?"
- "Can you describe the current data literacy and culture within the finance department? Are they actively using dashboards and data tools, or is this an area where a data analyst could help drive adoption and education?"
- "What's the relationship like between Finance and Operations? Is data a common language that bridges these two departments?"

---

## Questions for the S&OP Director

These questions are geared toward understanding the operational side, the day-to-day use of data, and the influence you'll have on supply chain and demand planning.

- "How is data currently being used to forecast demand and manage supply chain variability? What are the biggest pain points or inaccuracies you're looking to solve?"
- "Could you share a recent example of a major operational decision that was made using data? What data sources were used, and what was the outcome?"
- "What does the ideal data flow look like from the shop floor or sales team to your S&OP meetings? Where are the current bottlenecks in getting timely, accurate data?"
- "I'm passionate about the intersection of data and operations. How does this role collaborate with other teams like sales, marketing, and logistics to ensure a unified view of the business?"

---

## Technical Questions

These questions are designed to get a sense of their technical stack, data maturity, and how you'll operate on a daily basis.

- "What does the current **data architecture and tool stack** look like? For example, are you using SQL Server, BigQuery, or something else for your data warehouse, and is Tableau, Power BI, or another tool used for visualization?"
- "Can you walk me through the typical **data pipeline** for a new request? What's the process for a business user to request a new dashboard or report, from data extraction to final delivery?"
- "How are data quality and data governance managed within the organization? Do you have established processes for **data validation and security**?"
- "What's the process for deploying a new data model or report into production? Is there a version control system in place, and what does the **code review process** look like?"

---

## General & Closing Questions

These questions tie everything together and show your long-term vision and commitment.

- "From your perspective, what does success look like for the person in this role in their first six months?"
- "What kind of support and resources can I expect to have, in terms of tools, training, and access to key stakeholders, to be successful?"
- "Based on our conversation today, are there any concerns you have about my background or experience that I could clarify for you?" (This is a powerful, confident question that shows you're not afraid to tackle potential weaknesses head-on.)

Asking these types of questions demonstrates that you're not just looking for a job—you're looking for a partnership where you can use your skills to genuinely influence the business. Good luck! 🚀
