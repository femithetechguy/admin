# Technical Skills for 8+ Years of Experience

Based on the provided image, a BI professional with 8+ years of experience is expected to have mastered technical skills and, importantly, have moved into a leadership and architectural role. The expectations for this level are a combination of deep technical expertise and strategic, project-level management.

---

## 1. Project Management

A senior professional is no longer just a developer; they are expected to manage the entire BI project lifecycle. This includes:

- **SRS (Software Requirements Specification):** The ability to gather and document detailed project requirements.
- **Design:** Expertise in designing the overall BI solution and architecture.
- **Licensing:** A solid understanding of the licensing models for Power BI and other relevant tools.
- **Estimation:** The ability to accurately estimate project scope, timelines, and resources.
- **Team Handling:** Skills in leading and mentoring a team of BI developers and analysts.

---

## 2. Good Understanding of SQL & Data Warehousing

While these are foundational skills, a senior professional's knowledge is expected to be comprehensive. This includes:

- **SQL:** A thorough understanding of database management, advanced SQL queries, stored procedures, and performance tuning.
- **Data Warehousing:** Expertise in designing **dimensional models**, differentiating between **fact** and **dimension** tables, and managing **SCD (Slowly Changing Dimensions)**.

---

## 3. ETL Tool

At this level, the focus on ETL goes beyond just development. It also includes strategic planning and automation.

- **SSIS & Azure Data Factory:** Expertise in using these tools to build and manage complex data pipelines.
- **Automation:** The ability to automate repeatable tasks and ensure efficient data movement.
- **Reconciliation:** Experience in reconciling and validating report data against the source to ensure accuracy.

---

## 4. Business Intelligence

This requires a holistic understanding of the entire BI lifecycle, from start to finish. This includes:

- **Complete BI Module:** The ability to oversee a project from the initial requirements gathering to the final delivery and ongoing maintenance.

---

## 5. Power BI Architecture

A senior professional is expected to have a deep, architectural-level understanding of the Power BI ecosystem. This includes:

- **Core Skills:** Mastery of Power Query, modeling, relationships, and service deployment.
- **Advanced Features:** Expertise in **Power BI Premium** features, **Paginated Reports** (for print-ready reports), and **Embedded Analytics** for integrating reports into other applications.
- **Licensing:** A detailed understanding of the different licensing models (e.g., Pro, Premium per User, Premium Capacity).

---

## 6. In-depth Understanding of DAX & Modeling

DAX and modeling skills are at an expert level, focusing on complex scenarios and optimization.

- **Advanced DAX:** Mastery of date calculations, `CALCULATE`, and context transition, with a focus on performance improvement.
- **Modeling:** Expertise in building robust data models, including the use of **aggregations** to improve query performance on large datasets and **Composite Models** for integrating different data sources. Also, the ability to implement and manage **RLS (Row-Level Security)**.

---

## 7. SSAS & Azure Technologies

A senior professional **must have** **SSAS (SQL Server Analysis Services)** knowledge, with the **Tabular model** being the recommended choice for modern BI solutions. Furthermore, a **sound knowledge** of the broader **Azure data ecosystem** is essential, including:

- **Azure SQL Database & Azure Data Factory:** For data storage and integration in the cloud.
- **Azure Synapse & Azure Analysis Services:** For large-scale data warehousing and semantic modeling.
