S&OP stands for **Sales and Operations Planning**. It is a business management process that aims to align a company's various departments—such as sales, marketing, operations, finance, and product development—to ensure that supply and demand are balanced.

The core purpose of S&OP is to create a single, unified operational plan that all parts of the organization can follow. This process helps companies to:

* **Balance Supply and Demand:** It ensures that what the sales team is promising can be realistically produced and delivered by the operations team.
* **Improve Communication:** It breaks down departmental silos and fosters cross-functional collaboration.
* **Enhance Decision-Making:** By providing a single set of numbers and a clear plan, executives can make more informed, strategic decisions.
* **Optimize Resources:** It helps to manage inventory levels, production capacity, and financial resources more efficiently.

The S&OP process is typically a recurring, monthly cycle that involves several key steps:

1.  **Data Gathering/Forecasting:** Collecting and analyzing data on past sales, market trends, and internal factors to create a sales forecast.
2.  **Demand Planning:** The sales and marketing teams review the forecast and make adjustments based on promotions, new product launches, or other market intelligence.
3.  **Supply Planning:** The operations and supply chain teams assess their capacity to meet the demand plan, identifying any potential constraints or gaps.
4.  **Pre-S&OP Meeting:** Key stakeholders from different departments meet to review the plans, resolve any discrepancies, and create a reconciled plan.
5.  **Executive S&OP Meeting:** Senior leadership reviews the final plan and makes any necessary trade-off decisions, giving their official approval to move forward.
